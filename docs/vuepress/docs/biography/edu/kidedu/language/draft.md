🎬 完整对话脚本（边吃水果边讲）

Mama:​ Baby, you just ate a yummy strawberry. Do you know where it's going?

（宝贝，你刚吃了一颗美味的草莓。你知道它去哪儿了吗？）

Baby:​ It's in my tummy!

（在我肚子里！）

Mama:​ That's right! But guess what — it's going on a trip inside your body! Want to follow it?

（对！但是你知道吗——它要在你身体里进行一次旅行！想跟着看看吗？）

Baby:​ Yes! Where does it go first?

（想！它先去哪儿？）

Mama:​ First, your teeth chop it up — crunch crunch! Then your tongue helps you swallow. Down the slide it goes — whoosh!

（首先，你的牙齿把它切碎——咔嚓咔嚓！然后舌头帮你吞下去。沿着滑梯下去——嗖！）

Baby:​ Where's the slide?

（滑梯在哪儿？）

Mama:​ It's inside your neck! It's called a food pipe. The strawberry slides all the way down to your tummy. Gurgle gurgle!

（在你脖子里面！它叫食物管道。草莓一路滑到你的胃里。咕噜咕噜！）

Baby:​ What happens in the tummy?

（胃里发生什么？）

Mama:​ Your tummy is like a blender! It mixes the strawberry into a mushy soup. Then it goes into tiny twisty tubes that take the good stuff to make you strong.

（你的胃像一个搅拌机！它把草莓搅拌成糊状的汤。然后它进入细细弯弯的管子，那些管子把好东西拿走，让你变强壮。）

Baby:​ And then?

（然后呢？）

Mama:​ The parts we don't need go into a big tube and turn into... POOPOO!​ 💩

（我们不需要的部分进入一条大管子，变成……便便！）

Baby:​ Eww! Poopoo!

（噫！便便！）

Mama:​ That's right! And when it's ready, you sit on the potty and push push — out it comes! The strawberry's trip is all done!

（对呀！当它准备好了，你坐到小马桶上"嗯嗯"——它就出来啦！草莓的旅行结束啦！）

🎬 5岁版完整对话：食物的探险之旅

Mama:​ Sweetie, you just ate a banana. Do you want to know what happens to it inside your body?

（宝贝，你刚吃了一根香蕉。想知道它在你身体里发生了什么吗？）

Baby:​ Yeah! It goes to my tummy!

（想！它去我的肚肚里！）

Mama:​ Right! But your body is like a long adventure park for food. Want to be a tour guide and follow it?

（对！但你的身体对食物来说就像一个大型探险乐园。想当导游跟着它看看吗？）

Baby:​ Yes! Let's go!

（想！走吧！）

Mama:​ Step 1 — Mouth!​ 🦷

Your teeth chop the banana into tiny pieces. Your tongue mixes it with spit. Then... gulp! You swallow it!

（第一步——嘴巴！你的牙齿把香蕉切成小块。舌头把它和口水混合。然后……咕咚！你吞下去了！）

Baby:​ Where does it go next?

（接下来去哪儿？）

Mama:​ Step 2 — Esophagus!​ 🎢

It goes down a long tube called the esophagus. It's like a slide in your throat! Whoosh! Down it goes!

（第二步——食道！它沿着一条叫"食道"的长管子下去。就像你喉咙里的一条滑梯！嗖！下去了！）

Baby:​ Cool!

（酷！）

Mama:​ Step 3 — Stomach!​ 🌀

The banana lands in your stomach. Your stomach has strong muscles. It squeezes and mixes — gurgle gurgle! — turning the banana into a mushy soup!

（第三步——胃！香蕉落到你的胃里。你的胃有强壮的肌肉。它挤压、搅拌——咕噜咕噜！——把香蕉变成糊状的汤！）

Baby:​ Like a blender!

（像一个搅拌机！）

Mama:​ Exactly! Step 4 — Small Intestine!​ 🧪

The mushy soup flows into the small intestine. It's a very long, twisty tube — about as long as you are tall! It takes out all the nutrients — the good stuff that makes you grow big and strong.

（没错！第四步——小肠！糊糊汤流进小肠。它是一条很长很弯的管子——差不多和你一样高！它把所有的营养都提取出来——就是让你长高长壮的好东西。）

Baby:​ Wow, as tall as me?

（哇，和我一样高？）

Mama:​ Yep! Step 5 — Large Intestine!​ 💩

Whatever your body doesn't need goes into the large intestine. It's thicker and shorter. It squeezes out the water and turns the rest into poopoo!

（对！第五步——大肠！身体不需要的东西进入大肠。它更粗更短。它把水挤出来，把剩下的变成便便！）


Mama:​ Step 6 — Rectum and Anus!​ 🚽

The poopoo waits in the rectum — like a little waiting room. When it's ready, your anus opens and... push push! Out it comes! All done!

（第六步——直肠和肛门！便便在直肠里等着——就像一个等候室。准备好了，肛门打开……嗯嗯！它就出来啦！全部完成！）

Baby:​ That's so cool! The banana went on a whole adventure!

（太酷了！香蕉经历了一次完整的探险！）

Mama:​ It sure did! Your body is amazing, right?

（确实！你的身体很神奇，对吧？）
